隱私政策

本隱私政策說明「LinguaChase」（以下稱「本 App」）如何蒐集、使用、儲存與保護你的個人資料。本 App 目前僅提供 iOS 版本。

開發者：個人開發者 Alexander Tseng（[待補：完整名稱／聯絡 email]）。

若你不同意本政策，請勿註冊或使用本 App。

1. 適用範圍

本政策適用於你透過本 App 提供或產生的所有個人資料，包括帳號資料、學習紀錄、上傳內容與互動紀錄。本 App 僅供 iOS 使用，無網頁版。

2. 我們蒐集的資料

我們蒐集以下類別的資料：

  • 帳號資料：電子郵件、密碼（經加密儲存，我們無法讀取明文）、顯示名稱、所在地區。
  • 考試設定資料：目標考試（IELTS／TOEFL／TOEIC）、預計考試日期、每日練習時間、自評分數。
  • 學習紀錄：作答內容、分數、單字／技能掌握度、錯題複習項目、讀書計劃。
  • 作文內容：你提交的寫作練習與 AI 批改結果。
  • 口說錄音：模擬考中的語音錄音，僅本人登入後可播放，帳號刪除時一併刪除。
  • 聊天訊息：自習室聊天（登入使用者皆可見）、AI 家教對話（僅本人可見）。
  • 好友關係：你透過 6 碼邀請碼建立的好友連結。
  • 意見回饋：你主動提交的問題回報或建議。

目前本 App 不蒐集推播通知裝置權杖（push token）。

3. 資料如何被使用

我們使用上述資料以：提供做題、模擬考、單字、讀書計劃、AI 批改／評分／家教、考官語音朗讀、一日一新聞、自習室與好友等核心功能；計算學習掌握度與下一步建議；處理你的意見回饋；維護帳號安全與服務穩定。

4. 第三方服務與資料分享

我們使用以下第三方服務處理你的資料，且僅為提供本 App 功能之目的：

  • Google Gemini API：作文批改、口說評分、AI 家教對話會將相關文字／錄音內容傳送至 Google Gemini API 處理；本服務目前使用 Gemini API 免費層，Google 可能依其條款使用內容改進服務；日後若改用付費層，將更新本政策。
  • Fish Audio：僅傳送「考官語音」功能中要朗讀的題目文字，不會傳送你的錄音或任何個人資料。
  • Apple：App 內購買與訂閱由 Apple 處理，你的付款資訊（卡號等）由 Apple 收集與保管，我們不會取得或儲存你的付款資料；退款依 Apple 政策辦理。
  • Supabase：作為本 App 的資料庫、身分驗證與檔案儲存服務商，代管上述所有資料。

我們不會將你的個人資料出售或用於與本 App 功能無關的第三方行銷用途。

5. 資料儲存地點

你的資料透過 Supabase 儲存，資料庫所在區域為 日本東京（AWS ap-northeast-1）。

6. 資料保存期限

你的資料將保存至你刪除帳號為止。帳號刪除後，我們會於 30 天內清除或匿名化你的個人資料；其中口說錄音將優先刪除。若你於刪除帳號前已在自習室發表訊息，該訊息可能仍會以去識別化方式保留於他人對話紀錄中。

7. 未成年人使用

若你未滿 18 歲，須取得父母或法定監護人同意後方可使用本 App；若我們知悉未成年使用者未經同意使用本服務，將採取合理措施刪除相關帳號與資料。

8. 你的權利

依中華民國個人資料保護法，你就我們持有之個人資料享有查閱、複製、補充、更正、刪除等權利。你可於 App 內「我的」頁面直接刪除帳號與資料，或透過下方聯絡方式提出請求。我們將於合理期間內回覆並處理。

9. 資料安全

我們採取合理之技術與管理措施（如加密傳輸、存取權限控管）保護你的個人資料，但無法保證絕對安全；若發生資料外洩等重大事件，我們將依法通知受影響使用者與主管機關。

10. 政策修訂

我們可能不時修訂本政策，修訂後將更新本頁「最後更新」日期，重大變更將於 App 內另行通知。

11. 準據法與管轄

本政策之解釋與適用，以中華民國（台灣）法律為準據法，並適用個人資料保護法；因本政策所生之爭議，以台灣台北地方法院為第一審管轄法院。

12. 聯絡我們

如對本政策有任何疑問，或欲行使個人資料相關權利，請透過 [待補：聯絡 email] 與我們聯繫。

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how "LinguaChase" ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data. The App is currently available on iOS only.

Developer: Independent developer Alexander Tseng ([TBD: full legal name / contact email]).

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not register for or use the App.

1. Scope

This policy applies to all personal data you provide or generate through the App, including account data, learning records, submitted content, and interaction history. The App is iOS-only; there is no web version. This policy does not cover third-party services you access outside the App, such as the App Store itself, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:

  • Account data: email address, password (stored encrypted; we cannot read it in plain text), display name, region.
  • Exam profile: target exam (IELTS/TOEFL/TOEIC), planned exam date, daily practice minutes, self-reported scores.
  • Learning records: your answers, scores, vocabulary/skill mastery, review items for missed questions, study plans.
  • Essays: writing practice you submit and the AI grading results.
  • Speaking recordings: audio recorded during mock speaking tests; playable only by you while logged in, and deleted together with your account.
  • Chat messages: lounge chat (visible to all logged-in users) and AI tutor conversations (private to you).
  • Friend relations: connections made via a 6-digit invite code.
  • Feedback: issue reports or suggestions you submit voluntarily.

The App does not currently collect push-notification device tokens; if push notifications are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated before the relevant data is collected.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data above to: provide practice questions, mock tests, vocabulary, study plans, AI essay grading, AI speaking grading, AI tutor chat, examiner voice playback, daily news, lounge chat, and friends; compute mastery and next-step recommendations; process your feedback; and maintain account security and service reliability. We do not use your learning records, essays, or recordings to build user profiles for advertising, and we do not run third-party ad networks inside the App.

4. Third Parties and Data Sharing

We use the following third-party services, solely to provide the App's features:

  • Google Gemini API: essay grading, speaking grading, and AI tutor conversations send the relevant text/recording content to the Google Gemini API for processing. The service currently uses the Gemini API free tier, and Google may use the content to improve its services in accordance with its terms; this policy will be updated if we move to the paid tier.
  • Fish Audio: for the "examiner voice" feature, only the question text to be read aloud is sent — your recordings and other personal data are never sent.
  • Apple: in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. Your payment information (e.g., card numbers) is collected and held by Apple; we do not receive or store it. Refunds are handled per Apple's policy.
  • Supabase: our database, authentication, and file storage provider, which hosts all the data listed above.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party marketing unrelated to the App's features.

5. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored via Supabase, in the region Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1).

6. Data Retention

We retain your data until you delete your account. After deletion, we will erase or anonymize your personal data within 30 days; speaking recordings are deleted first. Lounge messages you posted before deletion may remain, in de-identified form, within other users' chat history.

7. Minors

If you are under 18, you must obtain consent from a parent or legal guardian before using the App. If we become aware that a minor is using the App without such consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete the related account and data.

8. Your Rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, copy, supplement, correct, and delete the personal data we hold about you, and to request that we stop processing or using it. You can delete your account and data directly from the App's "我的" (Me) screen, or submit a request via the contact details below. We will verify your identity before acting on a request that comes through channels other than the App itself, and will respond and act within a reasonable time.

9. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and administrative measures (e.g., encryption in transit, access controls, and relying on Supabase's managed authentication for password storage) to protect your personal data, but no method is completely secure. Access to your data within our systems is limited to what is necessary to operate and support the App. In the event of a significant data breach, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised accordingly, and material changes will be announced in-App.

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of China (Taiwan), including the Personal Data Protection Act. Disputes arising from this policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Taipei District Court, Taiwan.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at [TBD: contact email]. We will try to respond promptly and, where a request requires more time to complete, will let you know the expected timeline.
